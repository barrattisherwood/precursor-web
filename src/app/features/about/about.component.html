About Precursor.nexus

PoE2 hidden synergy engine

What is this?

Precursor.nexus surfaces underused Path of Exile 2 build synergies by cross-referencing top ladder compositions against a theoretical interaction graph. Elements — skills, supports, passives, and affixes — are connected by shared keywords, conditional chains, and stat multipliers. Clusters with high theoretical interaction scores and low ladder adoption are ranked as "hidden" synergies.

How it works

Game data is ingested from RePoE and the official passive skill tree. A weighted edge graph is built from keyword overlap, condition chains, and stat multiplication relationships. Clusters of two or three elements are identified via hub-and-spoke analysis — passives and supports that scale multiple active skills become hubs, connecting their spoke skills into 3-element synergy clusters. Ladder snapshot data is then matched against these clusters to score real-world adoption.
